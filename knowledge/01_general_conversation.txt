TITLE: GENERAL CONVERSATION AND ASSISTANCE

PURPOSE:
To define how the AI handles everyday chat, explains concepts, and interacts with users safely and helpfully.

WHAT IS IT?
General conversation covers the basic ways you communicate with the AI. This includes saying hello, asking for summaries, rewriting text, brainstorming ideas, comparing topics, and making plans.

WHY IS IT USEFUL?
A good AI assistant should feel natural to talk to. Before getting into complex programming, you often need help understanding a simple fact, organizing your thoughts, or breaking down a big topic into smaller pieces.

CORE CONCEPTS:

Greetings: Starting a conversation politely and clearly.

Explaining Concepts: Breaking down hard ideas using simple words or analogies.

Summarization: Making long text shorter while keeping the main points.

Rewriting: Changing the tone of text (e.g., making it more professional or simpler).

Brainstorming: Generating lists of ideas for projects or writing.

Comparisons: Looking at the pros and cons of two different things side-by-side.

Planning: Organizing tasks into a step-by-step checklist.

Factual Questions: Answering basic "who, what, when, where" questions.

Honesty about Uncertainty: Admitting when the AI does not know the answer.

SIMPLE EXPLANATION:
When you talk to the AI, you give it a "prompt." The AI reads the prompt, processes what you want, and gives back a response. If you want a summary, it gives you a short version. If you want ideas, it gives you a list.

EXAMPLES:

Brainstorming Example:
User: "Give me 3 simple Python project ideas."
AI:

A to-do list application.

A random password generator.

A simple text-based calculator.

Summarization Example:
User: "Summarize the water cycle in one sentence."
AI: "The water cycle is the continuous movement of water on, above, and below the surface of the Earth."

COMMON MISTAKES:

Prompting too vaguely: Asking "Tell me about code" is too broad. Asking "Tell me about Python lists" is much better.

Assuming the AI is always right: AI can make mistakes (called "hallucinations"). Always test code and verify important facts.

Forgetting to provide context: If you want the AI to rewrite an email, you must provide the original email text.

WHEN TO USE:
Use general conversation when you are exploring a topic, looking for inspiration, studying, or need help organizing your workflow.

WHEN NOT TO USE:
Do not use general AI chat for highly secure data (like real passwords or personal identity numbers). The AI is a helper, not a secure vault.

PROJECT CONNECTION:
In the AI Creator Engine, general conversation happens in the Chat flow (browser -> /api/chat). The AI reads your message, checks the Knowledge Base, and uses a model to generate a helpful response.

REMEMBER:
The AI does not "know" things like a human does. It predicts the best text to follow your prompt. Always ask it to explain its reasoning if you are confused!
